BankHub – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

BankHub is a private application operated by an individual in Spain (the "Operator"): [YOUR NAME]. Contact for data protection matters: [YOUR EMAIL].

2. What BankHub is

BankHub is a self-hosted personal finance tool. It is used only by its Operator to view the Operator's own bank accounts in one place. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

3. Data we access

With the Operator's explicit consent at their bank, BankHub reads, through the Enable Banking account information service: the list of accounts, account identifiers (IBAN), balances and transactions. Access is read-only. BankHub does not initiate payments.

4. Purpose

The data is used solely to manage the Operator's own personal finances: dashboards, categorisation of transactions and answering the Operator's own questions about their data.

5. Where data is stored and how it is protected

Data is stored in a database hosted on the Operator's own infrastructure. Access tokens are stored encrypted, the application is reachable only through a private network, and IBANs are masked wherever they are displayed.

6. Sharing

Data is not sold, used for advertising or analytics, or shared with third parties, except:

  • Enable Banking and the Operator's banks, which take part in the connection itself;
  • an AI assistant chosen by the Operator, which may receive the results of the Operator's own queries when the Operator asks it a question about their finances.

7. Retention and consent

Data is kept until the Operator deletes it. Bank consents expire according to each bank's rules and must be renewed by the Operator. The Operator can revoke a consent at any time with the bank, and can delete all stored data at any time.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your data. Because BankHub processes only its Operator's own data, these requests are handled by the Operator at the address above.
